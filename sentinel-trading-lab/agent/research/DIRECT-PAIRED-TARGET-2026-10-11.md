# Sentinel: direct future-entry comparison, 2026-10-11

Objective: forecast the direction from the future entry price T+H to the future expiry price T+2H. This differs from the direction from T to T+H. The latest endpoint/flow experiments must not be used as evidence for the paired future-entry task.

Baseline: original 13.4.67 commit `5c20afbef3060d00a5caa52cf0498aebff6b20c9`, Automatic mode. Nine locally imported core files were verified byte-for-byte against their Git blob hashes. No original core file was modified.

Candidate: learn paired returns directly from completed similar historical trajectories. Each retained historical outcome window is disjoint. Features use historical seconds, and delay is separate from holding duration. Neither future quotes nor incompletely resolved historical outcomes enter the forecast. The representative path is an observed historical example, not an independently median-smoothed fictitious trajectory. It is illustrative and not an exact future trajectory.

## Validation

Five Node tests passed, including a rise before entry followed by a fall during the holding interval, future-quote pollution, odd-second delay with independent holding duration, missing-history behavior, and the chronological paired benchmark.

Synthetic benchmark: 768 evaluated opportunities on eight new seeds, eight regimes, two issue times, and horizons 5/10/30/60/120/300 seconds. These are correlated observations, not real broker trades.

On the SAME 567 occasions where both versions emit a direction:

- 67: 368/567 (64.9%).
- Direct trajectory: 365/567 (64.37%).
- Rescued errors: 77; harmed previously correct forecasts: 80.
- Candidate coverage: 567/768 (73.83%).
- At 300 seconds the candidate has insufficient disjoint completed examples in the production-sized 2400-quote buffer and emits no direction. This is a FAILURE to cover the horizon, not a success or a reason to ignore that horizon.
- At 30 seconds: baseline 66.41%, candidate 67.97%. This small synthetic gain does not establish improvement or 80% performance.
- At 60 seconds: baseline 67.19%, candidate 67.97%.

## Decision

REJECTED for promotion. The candidate does not beat 67 across the complete benchmark, does not reach the user's 80% objective, and fails long-horizon coverage. Software contract tests passing do not mean forecasting efficacy passed.

No deployment, agent release, manifest, installer, UI, automatic orders, account access, or database operation. Production 67 is preserved.

Next valid evidence: chronological local broker quote journals with usable entry and expiry observations, evaluated against exact 67. Do not substitute external exchange prices for IQ Option OTC, or score the current-to-horizon direction as the future-entry operation.
